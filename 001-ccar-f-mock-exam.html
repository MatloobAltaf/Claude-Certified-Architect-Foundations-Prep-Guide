Before you begin

  1. Closed book. The real exam is proctored, so no notes, docs, or second tabs.
  2. Answer every question. Unanswered questions score as incorrect on submission.
  3. Flag anything you want to revisit; the answer sheet tracks flags and progress.
  4. Progress auto-saves in your browser after every answer, so moving between questions, closing the file, or refreshing never loses your work. (If a preview sandbox blocks saving, progress lasts for the session — keep that tab open.)
  5. Answers and explanations stay hidden until you submit (timed mode).
